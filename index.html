ACADEMIC PROFILE

AI for intelligent mobility & autonomous systems

My research focuses on artificial intelligence and machine learning for intelligent transportation systems, vehicular networks, and autonomous driving. I am especially interested in problems that arise in dynamic environments, where vehicles, infrastructure, and learning-based systems need to interact reliably and make decisions under constantly changing conditions.

A large part of my recent work has been centered on VANETs and intelligent mobility, with a particular focus on clustering, cluster-head selection, network stability, and learning-based methods for improving communication and overall system performance.

More broadly, I am interested in reinforcement learning, road safety, vision-language-action models, and AI for autonomous and connected systems. I am most drawn to research questions that are not only technically interesting, but can also be evaluated carefully and developed toward practical use in real-world mobility environments.
